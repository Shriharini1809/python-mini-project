🎯 Guess the Number – User Guide

Welcome to the Guess the Number game! Your goal is simple — guess the secret number within the available attempts.

🕹️ How to Play

1. Choose a Difficulty Level

       • 🟢 Easy – Number between 1 and 50, with 10 attempts.

       • 🟡 Medium – Number between 1 and 100, with 7 attempts.

       • 🔴 Hard – Number between 1 and 200, with 5 attempts.

2. Enter Your Guess

       • Enter a number within the selected range.

       • Click Guess Number to submit your guess.

3. Use the Hints

       • ✅ Right – You guessed the correct number.

       • 🔥 Hot – Your guess is close to the secret number.

       • ❄️ Cold – Your guess is far from the secret number.

4. Watch Your Attempts

       • Your remaining attempts are displayed below the game.

       • The attempts indicator turns red when fewer than 3 attempts remain.

5. Win or Lose

       • Guess the correct number before your attempts run out to win 🎉.

       • If all attempts are used, the game ends 😢.

🔄 Start a New Game

       After the game ends, choose a difficulty level again to start a new game with a new secret number.

💡 Tip

       Use the Hot and Cold hints to adjust your next guess and improve your chances of winning!
